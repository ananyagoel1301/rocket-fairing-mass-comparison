INTERPRETATION AND MODEL LIMITATIONS

The modified design has a lower initial and dry mass because the fairing
mass used in the model decreases from 332.38 kg to 172.52 kg, while the
propellant mass is held constant. Under this idealised model, the modified
design reaches a greater burnout altitude and velocity.

This is a simplified vertical, one-dimensional powered-ascent model. It
does not represent a complete launch vehicle trajectory or include staging,
steering, changing thrust, or structural and stability constraints. The
atmospheric density is set to zero above 84.852 km geopotential altitude;
therefore, the model neglects atmospheric drag above that cutoff. Results
should be treated as model estimates, not measured flight performance.
